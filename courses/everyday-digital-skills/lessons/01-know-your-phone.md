Your phone is a small computer, and a few settings make it faster, safer and cheaper to use. This lesson takes about 15 minutes. Keep your phone in your hand and try each step as you read.

## Find the Settings

Every phone has a **Settings** app (a gear or cog icon). Open it now. Most of what follows is in there. Phones differ a little, so if a name is not exactly the same, look for the nearest one or use the search box at the top of Settings.

## Lock your screen

If your phone is lost or stolen, a screen lock keeps your messages, photos and money apps private.

- Go to **Settings → Security** (or **Lock screen**).
- Choose a **PIN** of at least 6 digits, or a fingerprint or face unlock *with* a PIN behind it.
- Avoid easy PINs such as 123456, 000000 or your birth year.

> **Tip:** A pattern drawn on the screen can often be guessed from the smudges your finger leaves. A PIN is safer.

## Keep it up to date

Updates fix security holes that criminals use. Go to **Settings → System → Software update** (or **About phone → Software update**) and install what is waiting. Do it on Wi-Fi if you can, because updates can be large.

Update your apps too: open the **Play Store** (Android) or **App Store** (iPhone), go to your profile, and choose **Manage apps** or **Updates**.

## Make space

A phone that is nearly full becomes slow and stops taking photos and messages.

- **Settings → Storage** shows what is using space.
- Delete apps you no longer use.
- In WhatsApp, **Settings → Storage and data → Manage storage** shows large videos you can delete.
- Back up photos you want to keep (to a computer, a memory card, or a cloud service) before deleting them from the phone.

## Look after the battery

- Lower the screen brightness, or turn on **automatic brightness**.
- Turn on **battery saver** when you are below 20%.
- Close apps you are not using; turn off Bluetooth and location when you do not need them.
- Use a good charger. Very cheap chargers can damage the battery and the phone.

## Try it now

1. Check that your phone has a screen lock with a PIN.
2. Look at **Storage** and note how much space is free.
3. Check for one software or app update.

When you are done, mark this lesson complete and go on to saving data.
