When people search for "tailor near me" or "phone repair" on Google, they see businesses on a map. A free **Google Business Profile** puts your business there.

## What you need

- A Google account (a Gmail address).
- Your business name, category, phone number and, if customers visit you, an address they can find.

## Create the profile

1. Go to **google.com/business** and choose **Manage now**.
2. Type your business name and choose the category that fits best.
3. Say whether customers visit you. If you go to them (for example, a plumber), you can hide your address and list the areas you serve instead.
4. Add your phone number and, if you have one, a website or WhatsApp link.
5. Google asks you to **verify** that the business is yours. Follow the steps it shows; they differ from place to place and can take a few days.

## Make it useful

- **Opening hours**, kept up to date, including holidays.
- **Photos** of your shop front (so people can find it), inside, your products and your team.
- A short **description** of what you offer.

## Reviews

Customers can leave reviews. Ask happy customers to write one. Reply to every review politely, including bad ones: a calm, helpful reply to a complaint shows future customers you care. Never pay for fake reviews.

> **Note:** Google never asks you to pay to list a business. Ignore calls offering to "verify" or "rank" your listing for a fee.

## Try it now

Search for your type of business near you on Google Maps. Look at the top three: what do their profiles have that makes you want to choose them?
