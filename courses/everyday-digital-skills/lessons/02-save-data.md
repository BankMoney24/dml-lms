Data costs money. A few changes can make the same bundle last much longer, without missing anything important.

## Use Wi-Fi when you can

Wi-Fi at home, work, a church, a school or a business centre does not use your data bundle. When you connect, the phone remembers it for next time. Do big things on Wi-Fi: app updates, phone updates, backing up photos and downloading videos.

> **Warning:** Do not open your bank app or type passwords on Wi-Fi you do not trust, such as an open network with no password. Use your mobile data for those.

## Turn on data saver

- **Android:** Settings → Network and internet → **Data Saver**. Apps then use data in the background only when you allow them.
- **iPhone:** Settings → Mobile Data → Mobile Data Options → **Low Data Mode**.

## Stop apps eating data in the background

**Settings → Network → Data usage** (Android) or **Settings → Mobile Data** (iPhone) lists which apps used the most data this month. If an app you rarely open is near the top, turn off its background data or mobile data.

## WhatsApp, the biggest user for many people

In WhatsApp: **Settings → Storage and data → Media auto-download**. Set "When using mobile data" to **No media**, or only photos. Videos then download only when you tap them.

## Videos and music

- Watch videos at a lower quality: in YouTube, tap the settings gear on a video and choose a lower **Quality**, such as 360p.
- Download a video or a lesson on Wi-Fi and watch it later offline.

## Learning here without using much data

On this platform:

- Videos load only when you press play, and the page shows how big the video is first.
- Open a lesson and choose **Save for offline**. You can read it later with no connection at all, and anything you finish is sent when you are back online.

## Check your balance

Your network shows your data balance through its app or a short code. Check it once a week so the bundle does not run out at a bad time, and compare bundles: a monthly bundle is usually cheaper per gigabyte than daily ones.
