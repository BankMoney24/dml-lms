If you sell anything (food, clothes, repairs, tailoring, hair, farm produce) WhatsApp Business is a free shop window on your customers' phones.

## WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business?

WhatsApp Business is a separate free app from WhatsApp, made for small businesses. It can use a different number from your personal WhatsApp, which keeps business and family chats apart. Install it from the Play Store or App Store.

## Set up your profile

In WhatsApp Business: **Settings → Business tools → Business profile**. Fill in:

- **Name** of your business, exactly as customers know it.
- **Category**, for example Food and drink, or Beauty.
- **Description:** what you sell and where, in one or two sentences.
- **Address** and **hours**, if customers come to you.
- A clear **profile photo**: your logo, or your shop front.

## Add a catalogue

**Business tools → Catalogue → Add new item.** For each product: a good photo (next lesson), a name, the price, and a short description (size, colour, what is included). Customers can browse it and send you an item in one tap.

## Let it answer for you

- **Greeting message:** sent automatically to new customers. Say who you are and how to order.
- **Away message:** sent outside your opening hours, so nobody feels ignored.
- **Quick replies:** save answers you type often, such as your bank details or delivery prices, and send them by typing "/".

## Keep track with labels

Labels such as *New order*, *Paid*, *Delivered* help you see who is waiting for what.

## Use Status

Post new stock or today's special on your **Status**. Customers who saved your number see it, at no cost.

> **Important:** Only ask customers to pay into your business account, and check your own bank app for the money before you send goods (see the lesson on scams).

## Try it now

1. Fill in your business profile.
2. Write a greeting message and an away message.
3. Save one quick reply.
