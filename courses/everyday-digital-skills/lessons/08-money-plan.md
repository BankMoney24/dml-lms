Put what you learned into a plan for yourself or your family. This is for you: there are no wrong answers, and a teacher may read it and reply if you take this course with a group.

## Write down

1. **Your bank's official fraud number**, and where you saved it.
2. **Your daily transfer limit** now, and whether you changed it.
3. **Three things you will never share** with anyone who calls or messages you.
4. **What you will do in the first ten minutes** if money leaves your account that you did not send.
5. **One person** you will teach this to (a parent, child or friend) and when.

Type your plan in the box, or take a photo of a handwritten one and attach it.

> **Important:** Do not write your actual PIN, passwords, card numbers or BVN in your plan.
